What do these measurements tell us?

Sepals are the outer flower segments; petals are the inner segments. Petal measurements often help distinguish Iris species. Sample buttons only fill the form: the predicted species comes from your trained model after you submit.

This is a learning demo for the three Iris classes, not a general flower identification tool.
